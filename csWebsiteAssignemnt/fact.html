Quick Facts to Think About

Average screen time: Many adolescents spend 7–9 hours per day on screens outside of schoolwork (Journal of Education and Health Promotion, 2023).

Posture issues: Over 60% of young adults report neck or back pain linked to technology use (Dong & Wang, 2022).

Sleep disruption: Screen exposure before bed can delay sleep onset by up to an hour (Medical News Today, n.d.).

Health risks: Sedentary behavior increases the likelihood of obesity, type 2 diabetes, and cardiovascular disease (MDPI – Journal of Clinical Medicine, 2019).

Positive use: Wearable fitness trackers have been shown to increase daily step count and encourage regular exercise (Siddiqui & Alghamdi, 2022).
