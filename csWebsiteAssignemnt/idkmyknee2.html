INTRODUCTION

Over the years, technology has become an integral part of our life. It offers us convenience, entertainment, and connection with others. Phones, computers, and social media keep us informed, connected, and (ideally) productive. But, at the same time, the digital revolution brings a dilemma: Do these new technologies have any drawbacks? And can individuals enjoy them without harming their physical health? On one hand, tech makes it possible for us to track our fitness routine, we get access to online exercise programs, and we get more aware of our health. On the other hand, too much screen time can promote sedentary lifestyles, bad postuee, eye problems, bad sleep habits, and other long-term health riska. Health profesionals emphasiez the dangers of overusing tech, while enthusiats may view these devices as essential for daily life. So how managing their tech habits to balance these viewpoints is crucial for individuals' own benefits.
